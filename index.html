GoodLuck Fishing, the app

Here you fish a shared sky, gathered a few times a day. The app gives you a fleet of your own.

  • Live. Each time you sail, your boats read about 2,800 company job boards for your titles and your city.
  • Only open jobs. Before a letter bites, its posting is checked; a closed one is stamped and put away.
  • All on your computer. Your CV and your letters never leave it. No account. Free.
Download for Windows
